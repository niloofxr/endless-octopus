HOW TO PLAY

Keep Ollie moving through the open gaps.

Swim: press Space, click, or tap to swim upward. Avoid rocks, seaweed, wrecks, and the edges of the water.
Regular food: collect the tan and orange snacks. They make Ollie grow.
Sea-leaf snack: in Level 3, collect green leaves to shrink Ollie if he gets too large for a gate.
Hooked bait: in Levels 2 and 3, dodge bait with hooks. A fisherman’s net will scoop Ollie up.
★Try to get: safe food when you have room to grow, and sea-leaf snacks when Level 3’s gates start feeling tight.
